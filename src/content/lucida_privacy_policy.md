# Privacy Policy — Lucida

**Last updated: 18 September 2026**

Lucida ("the App") is operated by **GoGillion Technologies Private Limited** ("GoGillion", "we", "us", "our"), a company incorporated in India and based in Bengaluru. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the App collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. It is written in plain language to meet the requirements of the Apple App Store and Google Play and to give you a clear account of how we handle your data.

By creating a profile or using Lucida, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 2. Age requirement

Lucida is for adults. **You must be 18 years or older to create any profile**, including profiles you create for family members. We do not knowingly create profiles for, or process the personal data of, anyone under 18. If we learn that a profile was created for a minor, we will delete it.

## 3. What data we collect

We collect only what the App needs to generate your readings and operate the service.

**a. Birth details you provide**\
Your date of birth, time of birth, and place of birth. These are used to calculate your astrological chart, numerology, and timing signals.

**b. Your name (optional)**\
If you provide a name for a profile, it is used only to personalize your experience within the App. Your name is **never** sent to our third-party AI provider.

**c. Palm photo (optional)**\
If you choose to use palm analysis, your palm photo is checked for brightness and sharpness **on your device** before anything is uploaded. It is then analysed once to extract palmistry features — a structured description of lines, mounts, and markings — and is **discarded immediately after analysis**. The photo is never written to our database, never stored on disk, and never retained. Only the extracted features are stored, linked to that profile. Palm analysis is entirely optional; a profile works fully without it.

**d. Phone number (for sign-in)**\
We use your phone number to send a one-time password (OTP) for sign-in. We store a one-way cryptographic hash (HMAC) of your own phone number to prevent duplicate or fraudulent sign-ups. Our refer-a-friend feature is code-based: you share your own referral code, and a friend enters it when they sign up. **We never ask you for, or store, anyone else's phone number for referrals.**

**e. Your questions and conversations ("Ask Lucida")**\
The questions you ask and the conversations you have are stored so the App can maintain continuity across your questions.

**f. Usage analytics**\
We collect usage metadata only — which screens and features are used, plus basic technical details such as app version and platform. We do **not** collect your reading content, chat content, birth details, or palm features as part of analytics.

**g. Purchase records**\
When you subscribe or make a purchase, the transaction is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. **We do not store your payment card details on our servers.** We keep a record of your entitlement (what you purchased and when it is valid) to deliver the service.

## 4. How we use your data

We use your data to:

- calculate your chart and generate your readings, numerology, timing, and (if you opt in) palmistry;
- maintain continuity in your Ask Lucida conversations;
- authenticate you at sign-in via OTP;
- operate free-tier limits and Lucida Plus entitlements;
- improve the App through aggregate, non-identifying usage analytics;
- provide support and respond to your requests.

We do **not** sell your personal data, and we do **not** share it with advertisers.

---

## 5. Third parties who process your data

To deliver the service, limited data is shared with the following categories of service providers, who process it only on our instructions:

- **AI reading provider.** To generate your readings and answers, your birth details and extracted palm features (and the text of your questions) are sent to a third-party AI service. **Your name is never sent.** The AI service processes this input to return your reading.
- **SMS / authentication providers.** Your phone number is shared with our SMS provider (in India) or an authentication provider (internationally) solely to deliver your sign-in OTP.
- **Cloud hosting and database.** Your data is stored with our cloud database provider (MongoDB Atlas) and hosting infrastructure.
- **Geocoding.** Your place of birth may be converted to coordinates using a geocoding service to calculate your chart.
- **App stores.** Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store and Google Play under their own privacy policies.

Our service providers may change over time. We remain responsible for your data throughout, and we require our providers to protect it.

Some of these providers may process data on infrastructure located outside India, in accordance with applicable law.

---

## 6. How we protect your data

- Data is encrypted **in transit** using TLS/HTTPS between the App and our servers.
- Data **at rest** in our production database (MongoDB Atlas) is encrypted on disk.
- API keys and configuration are held in an encrypted vault accessible only to the app runtime.
- Your palm photo is never stored — only the extracted features are kept.
- Your name is never transmitted to the AI provider.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

---

## 7. How long we keep your data

We keep your profile data for as long as your profile exists, so we can generate and re-generate your readings. Extracted palm features are kept until you delete them or delete the profile. Ask Lucida conversations are kept to maintain continuity. Usage analytics are kept in aggregate form.

When you request account deletion, we action it within **30 days**.

---

## 8. Your rights and choices

You can, at any time:

- **Delete your palm features** for a profile (this affects only that profile);
- **Delete a profile** you added;
- **Update your birth details** going forward;
- **Request full account deletion** through in-app support, actioned within 30 days;
- **Turn off promotional notifications** in Settings (service notifications, such as a reading being ready, are separate);
- **Withdraw consent** for optional processing, such as palm analysis, by not using that feature or deleting its data.

Please note: Lucida offers deletion of whole profiles and accounts, and correction of birth details going forward. It does not offer selective deletion of individual past readings or individual chat questions.

You can correct your birth details or delete your data at any time using the controls above, and you can raise a grievance about how your data is handled by contacting us as described in Section 1.

## 9. The nature of Lucida's guidance

Lucida draws on astrology (Western, Vedic, Chinese), numerology, palmistry, and tarot-inspired interpretation. **All readings are reflective guidance and symbolic interpretation — not statements of fact or prediction.** They are designed to support your thinking, not replace it.

Lucida is **not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or mental-health advice.** For serious situations, please consult a qualified professional. Our AI guidance is designed to avoid deterministic predictions about health, death, or legal outcomes, to handle sensitive topics with care, to acknowledge uncertainty honestly, and to redirect you to professional help when a topic warrants it.
